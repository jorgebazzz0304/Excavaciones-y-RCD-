## Design Context

### Users
Construction project managers, farmers, agricultural businesses, and municipalities in the Miajadas/Extremadura region looking for excavation, RCD waste management, and transport services.

### Brand Personality
Flexible, communicative, quality-focused, prestigious. A family business since 1957 — trust and reliability are paramount.

### Aesthetic Direction
Professional and grounded. Light theme (users browse during work hours). Earthy, warm palette (gold/orange/green) reflecting construction and land. Clean, no-frills layout that conveys competence. Not flashy — solid and trustworthy.

### Design Principles
1. **Trust through clarity** — clean layout, readable text, easy navigation
2. **Local warmth** — approachable tone, family heritage emphasis
3. **Professional restraint** — no decorative excess, let the work photos speak
4. **Mobile-ready** — clients may browse from job sites on phones

### Quality Bar
Good-enough — functional polish, no flagship-level refinement needed.
